\begin{acknowledgments}

% This has been a long journey, over which Sau Lan has been a constant force propelling me forward.
It seems to me like yesterday when Sau Lan and I accidentally met outside her office in Chamberlin Hall and began a long journey together, over which she has been a constant 
force propelling me forward.
She took me under her wings, giving me guidance while allowing me to grow, explore different opportunities, and make key decisions on my career.
The path was not always propitious. As I went through setbacks and failures, Sau Lan has never given up on me, even when I was ready to do so. 
She continued to pave new paths leading me to success, and for that, I cannot overstate my gratitude.

The Wisconsin ATLAS team has also been a big supportive family that I am grateful to be a part of. 
I thank my senior colleagues Rui, Wasikul, Chen, Werner, Wen, and Shaojun for their help in my research activities, writing my thesis, and preparing for important presentations. 
To my fellow graduate students Jay, Alex and Alkaid, it has been a pleasure to work with and learn from all of you. 
I hope we could continue to collaborate in the future.

The Wisconsin family cannot be complete without Sylvie, she who actually runs this group. 
You have made my life so much easier, taking care of me, and helping me navigate through life in- and outside CERN. 
Je te remercie vraiment pour tout ce que tu as fait, de m'avoir aidé à bien installer dans une ville dont je ne parle presque pas la langue, d'être ma parente et mon amie au cours de ces dernières années. 

To my second family, the GNN4ITk group: Paolo, Jan, Daniel, Sylvain, Xiangyang, Alexis, Alina, Jay, Jared, Mark, Heberth, Christophe. 
You have been wonderful mentors and colleagues, who not only supported but also challenged me to become a better researcher. 
I especially thank Paolo for introducing me to this project, providing seemingly inlimited resources, and giving me valuable feedback, guidance and encouragement.
Daniel, you have been a special teacher, a collaborator, a supporter, and above all a friend with whom time spent together always seems too short.
Sylvain, merci d'avoir toujours été là pour moi, de m'avoir aidé dans toutes mes démarches, et d'avoir fait preuve de patience avec mon français cassé. 
Alexis, Alina, and Xiangyang, thank you all for always being the colleagues on whom I can count and to whom I owe the fruit of this thesis. 

To those who personally accompanied me through this journey, it would not have been possible without you in my life.
As in every endeavour I made, my parents and sister have given me unconditional support and strength to march forward. 
Khue, Sam and Khuyen, I cannot overstate how much our friendship means to me. 
Edward and Christopher, you are my best buddies and lifelong friends whom I count myself lucky to have. 
An, Minh, Lam be, I could never spend enough time with you guys, and I cherish every moment we see each another.
Chi Thu, chi My, merci à vous deux de m'avoir appris le français et d'être devenues mes chères amies. 
Kieu Chinh, who would have thought that someone we so randomly met could end up sharing so much joy and memories together like we do?
